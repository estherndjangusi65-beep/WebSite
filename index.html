Message from the Governor

Dear People of Ituri,

Welcome to the Ituri Provincial Government website. It is a pleasure to connect with you through this platform, which provides information about our province, its administration, public services and development activities.

Ituri is a province with great potential, rich cultural diversity and hardworking communities. Our shared responsibility is to promote peace, unity, security and sustainable development for the benefit of all citizens.

I encourage everyone to remain informed, participate in community development and work together towards a peaceful and prosperous Ituri.

Thank you for your continued cooperation and commitment to our province.

Major General Gaby Kasongo Mulumba Batoka
Military Governor of Ituri Province
