Major General Gaby Kasongo Mulumba Batoka

Governor of Ituri Province

The Governor is the head of the provincial executive and represents the provincial government in the administration of Ituri Province.

The Governor's leadership is important in promoting good governance, supporting development programmes and improving public services for communities across the province.

Responsibilities of the Governor

Leadership and Governance

Provides leadership to the provincial executive and supports the implementation of provincial policies and programmes.

Peace and Security

Works with relevant institutions and stakeholders to support peace, security and stability within the province.

Provincial Development

Promotes development initiatives in infrastructure, agriculture, education, health and other important sectors.

Public Services

Supports the improvement of public services and the well-being of the people of Ituri.

Building a Better Ituri

The development of Ituri requires cooperation between provincial institutions, local communities, development partners and citizens. Working together can help build a peaceful, stable and prosperous province.
