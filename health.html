Health

The Government of Ituri Province is committed to promoting access to healthcare, supporting disease prevention, and encouraging citizens to follow official health guidance to protect themselves, their families, and their communities.

Health in Ituri

The Government of Ituri Province works with health authorities, healthcare professionals, and development partners to support the delivery of essential health services across the province. These efforts aim to improve access to medical care and promote the well-being of individuals, families, and communities.

Our priority is to encourage disease prevention, strengthen community health awareness, and ensure that residents receive reliable health information. Through cooperation with health centres and local communities, we aim to promote a healthier and safer environment for everyone in Ituri Province.

Message to the community

Stay informed through official health authorities and follow their guidance on protecting your health and preventing the spread of diseases. If you or a family member feel unwell, especially with fever or other unusual symptoms, visit the nearest health centre promptly for medical attention. Participate in community health awareness campaigns and practise good hygiene to help protect yourself, your family, and the wider community.

What we do

🏥 Health centres

Supporting access to essential medical services, healthcare professionals, and treatment facilities to improve the health and well-being of communities across Ituri Province.

📢 Prevention campaigns

Promoting public awareness about disease prevention, personal hygiene, vaccination, and healthy practices to help families protect themselves and their communities.

🛡️ Ebola response

Supporting disease awareness, early reporting of suspected symptoms, and cooperation with health authorities and partners to help prevent the spread of Ebola and other infectious diseases.
