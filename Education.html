Education

Schools, enrolment, education programmes and learning opportunities for the children and youth of Ituri. The Government of Ituri Province is committed to supporting access to quality education, encouraging school enrolment, and promoting learning opportunities that help children and young people build a better future.

Education in Ituri

The provincial government works with education authorities, schools, and relevant partners to support learning opportunities for children and young people across Ituri Province. These efforts aim to encourage school attendance and improve access to education.

Our priority is to promote inclusive and accessible education, helping every child develop the knowledge and skills needed for a better future.

Message to parents and students

Parents and students are encouraged to contact the relevant school or provincial education authorities for information about enrolment procedures, required documents, school requirements, and available education programmes. Supporting regular school attendance helps children continue their learning and development.

What we do

🏫 Schools

Providing information about schools and supporting efforts to improve access to learning environments for children and young people throughout Ituri Province.

📝 Enrolment

Guiding parents and students on school registration procedures, enrolment requirements, and the information needed to begin or continue their education.

📚 Programmes

Promoting educational programmes and learning opportunities that help students develop academic knowledge, practical skills, and abilities for their future.
