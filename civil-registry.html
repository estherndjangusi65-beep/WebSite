Civil Registry and Documents

The Government of Ituri Province is committed to providing accessible civil registration services and helping citizens obtain the official documents they need. Citizens are encouraged to contact the relevant offices for information about registration procedures and required documents.

Civil Registry in Ituri

The civil registry records important life events, including births, marriages, and deaths, and provides official documents that serve as proof of these events. These records help protect citizens' identity and support access to essential administrative services.

Our priority is to make civil registration services clear, accessible, and efficient for every citizen in Ituri Province. We encourage residents to register important life events and contact the relevant offices for guidance on the procedures and documents required.

Message to citizens

The documents required and applicable fees depend on the type of service requested. Citizens are encouraged to contact the relevant office before visiting to confirm the required documents, procedures, and fees. This helps ensure a smoother and more efficient service experience.

What we do

👶 Birth certificates

Registration of births and issuance of birth certificates to help establish a child's identity and provide official proof of birth.

💍 Marriage certificates

Registration of marriages and issuance of marriage certificates as official records of a legally recognised marriage.

📄 Other documents

Information and guidance on other available administrative documents and the procedures required to obtain them.
