Provincial Administration

Supporting effective governance and public service delivery across Ituri Province.

About the Provincial Administration

The Provincial Administration is responsible for supporting the implementation of government policies, coordinating administrative activities and facilitating public services across Ituri Province.

It works with relevant government institutions, local authorities and communities to support effective administration and provincial development.

Main Responsibilities

Policy Implementation

Supports the implementation of provincial policies, decisions and government programmes.

Public Service Delivery

Helps coordinate services that support citizens and improve the delivery of public services.

Administrative Coordination

Facilitates cooperation between provincial institutions and relevant local authorities.

Development Support

Supports the coordination of programmes aimed at improving infrastructure and community well-being.

Key Areas of Administration

  • Provincial planning and coordination.
  • Public administration and institutional support.
  • Coordination of development programmes.
  • Cooperation with local authorities.
  • Support for public service delivery.

Working Together for Development

Effective administration requires cooperation between government institutions, local authorities, development partners and citizens. Their collaboration contributes to a more organised and better-served province.
