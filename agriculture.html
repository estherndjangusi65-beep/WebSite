Agriculture and Economy in Ituri

The provincial government supports farmers, traders, entrepreneurs, and local businesses to promote agricultural development and strengthen the economy of Ituri Province.

Our priority is to encourage productive agriculture, support business growth, and help producers and entrepreneurs access markets and economic opportunities.

Message to farmers, traders and investors

Farmers, traders, entrepreneurs, and potential investors are encouraged to contact the relevant provincial offices for information about support programmes, registration procedures, agricultural initiatives, and investment opportunities in Ituri Province.

What we do

🌾 Farmers

Promoting agricultural activities, supporting local producers, and encouraging improved farming practices that contribute to food production and rural livelihoods.

🛒 Traders and businesses

Encouraging local trade, entrepreneurship, and small business development to create economic opportunities and strengthen commercial activities across the province.

📈 Investors

Providing information about investment opportunities that can support economic growth, job creation, and community development in Ituri Province.
