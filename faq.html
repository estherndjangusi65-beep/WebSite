Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the Provincial Government and its services.

What is this website for?

This is the official website of the Provincial Government of Ituri. It brings the administration closer to citizens by sharing information on public services, development projects and news from across the province.

Which services can I find information about?

You can find information on civil registry and documents, health, education, and agriculture and the economy. Use the Services menu at the top of any page.

How can I contact the Provincial Government?

You can call, send an email or write to us using the form on the Contact page. The address, phone numbers and email are listed there and in the footer of every page.

Where is the Provincial Government located?

Our address is Mulunge Avenue, Lumumba Quarter, Mbunya Commune, Bunia, Ituri Province. The Contact page has a link to open it in Google Maps.

What documents and fees do I need for a civil registry request?

The documents and fees depend on the type of request. Please contact the office to confirm them before you visit.

Where can I find the latest news?

The latest news is on the News page, and headlines also scroll across the top of the home page.

Who is the Governor of Ituri?

The Military Governor of Ituri Province is Major General Gaby Kasongo Mulumba Batoka. His message to citizens is on the home page.

How can I share a suggestion?

We invite you to share your suggestions with us. Send a message using the form on the Contact page.
